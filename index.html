About

From assembly code to AI platforms

Technology leader with 30+ years in enterprise IT — application development, systems engineering, professional services, operations leadership, and data & AI platforms. Equally at home owning a CIO's remit (infrastructure, operations, security, P&L) and a CDO's (data strategy, governance, AI enablement).

Most recently Head of the Data Analytics Infrastructure Office at NHG (Deputy Director), where I directed the cluster's Analytics & AI portfolio — delivering a cluster-wide clinical data lakehouse on Databricks and AWS end to end: strategy, procurement, architecture, governance and adoption, with privacy-preserving analytics and an anonymisation framework I helped establish. Earlier leadership roles include Operations Director at CrimsonLogic (TradeNet, LawNet), GM at RadianTrust (winning SingPass II, TrustSG, and AVA SecurePrinting), and CTO at BEENET.

My foundation is deep and broad: application programming in finance and healthcare, enterprise systems support at HP (MPE, HP-UX), and Level 3 escalation and consulting at Sun Microsystems. That full-stack-of-the-org perspective — from the silicon up to the boardroom — is what I now bring to building modern, governed, AI-ready data platforms.

Currently deepening the craft with a Master of Science in Design and AI for Enterprise, on top of an MSc in Data Science (SUTD) — and doing hands-on AI engineering: cleaning training data, fine-tuning open models with SFT and QLoRA, and publishing deployable quantised models on Hugging Face.
